## The fix

See D-186.

- **Runtime** (`pts/runtime/src/scheduler.c`, `loop.c`, `scheduler_internal.h`, `pool.c`, `promise.c`, `http.c`, `include/pts.h`): a sleeping loop's timed wait until its first timer; `rejoin`, the compare-and-swap that raises the awake count only above zero; `run` running due timers itself and going on from poll after work done outside libuv (one `UV_RUN_DEFAULT` pass first, then `UV_RUN_NOWAIT` and `UV_RUN_ONCE` passes); stdout flushed before a loop sleeps; `now_of` for setting, refreshing and rescheduling timers and for arming the wait; a timer's and a server's loop from `pts_own_loop()`; `pts_loop_init` taking the loop before allocating; `pts_parallel_nesting` around a task's closure and each pts:parallel operation, and `pts_parallel_on_main_thread`.
- **Library** (`pts/lib/test.pts`): `requireMainThread` at the start of `test`, `it`, `describe`, `suite` and the four hooks; `it` and `suite` as functions of their own.
- **Compiler:** a comment in the generated `main` (`pts/cgen/cgen.go`).

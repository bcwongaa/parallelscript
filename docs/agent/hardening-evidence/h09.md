## The fix

D-190 has the design. In short:

- `pts/lower/closures.go`: `functionExpression` makes a `genericFunc` of a generic arrow function or function expression that isn't a module-scope const's, outside erased code and under `AllInstances`; once its erased value is recorded it takes its `scope` and `calledOnly`; `capture` records, in a trial, the captures the trial's code adds to functions outside it.
- `pts/lower/generics.go`: `genericFunc.closure`, `.scope`, `.calledOnly`; `scope`, `scopeOf`, `enterScope`, `calledOnlyName`; `declareInstance` makes a closure instance share its erased copy's closure; `instanceBody` lowers it in its scope; `genericInstance` counts what it reports as the trial's and marks the limit foreign; `genericInstanceFor`, `madeFor`, `maxDynamicVariants` (a dynamic result's call takes only an instance made for type arguments of the same `keyPart`s).
- `pts/lower/instances.go`: `demand.in`, `.callee`, `.dropped`; `erasedValue.dropped`; `addErasedValue` reports whether it recorded; `demandAt` takes dynamic results under `AllInstances`; `specialize` skips dropped pairs and pairs a called-only const only with its own calls; `demandAt` keys a dynamic result's demand by its type arguments, parameters and result (`instanceKey`, `keyPart`, `keyIdentity`, `isNominal`, `objectKeyParts`, `irTypeKeyPart`, `maxKeyDepth`, `maxKeyParts`, `lowerer.instanceKeys`); `tryInstance` makes a keyed demand's instance through `genericInstanceFor`; `tryInstance`, `trial`, `capturedBy`, `owns`, `lowerPending`; `shareCaptures`; `specializeMethod` uses `tryInstance`.
- `pts/lower/lower.go`: `Specialization` (`AllInstances`, `DeclaredInstances`), `ProgramWith`; `lowerer.specialization`, `.trial`, `.droppedFuncs`; `report` and `noteUnexplained` mark a trial foreign for other code; `shareCaptures` before `markCells`. `destructuring.go` uses `noteUnexplained`; `arrays.go`, `objects.go` and `optional.go` record each relaxed cast's function, and `checkRelaxedCasts` skips dropped ones.
- `pts/driver/driver.go`: `load` once, then `emitC`/`build` with `AllInstances` and, if that fails with compile or internal errors, `DeclaredInstances` (`fallsBack`); `EmitCWith`.
- `pts/ir/ir.go`, `print.go`: `Func.Shares`. `pts/cgen/closures.go`: `collectClosures` names the erased copy's closure struct, `capturePrologue` reads an instance's captures from it; `pts/cgen/classes.go`: `instanceTable` passes `pts_env` to a closure instance.
- `pts/runtime/src/convert.c`: `copy_tuple_into_array` (C70); C unit test `a_tuple_is_copied_into_an_array` (`convert_test.c`).
- Tests: the reproducer's `run/generic_arrow_factories.pts`, `generic_arrow_captures.pts`, `generic_method_factories.pts` (skipped, H09b), `generic_tuple_rest_results.pts`, `generic_values_any_boundaries.pts` (no-diff guard); the parent's `generic_factory_promises_generators.pts`, `generic_instances_that_dont_lower.pts` (no-diff), and after review round 1 `generic_arrow_contexts.pts`, `generic_arrow_suspensions.pts`, `generic_instances_keep_compiling.pts`, `generic_instances_fall_back.pts`, after round 2 `generic_dynamic_results_by_type.pts`, `generic_instances_unknown_type_arguments.pts`, after round 4 `generic_dynamic_results_outer_type_arguments.pts`, after round 5 `generic_dynamic_results_nested_dynamic_values.pts`, after round 6 `generic_dynamic_results_ir_identity.pts`; `generic_factories_fallbacks.pts`'s arrow lines now print the boxes (its expected output changed from the TypeErrors to Node's lines; the D-102 line keeps its TypeError); `lower_test.go`'s three tests, `driver_test.go`'s two; `TestCompilerFailsCleanly` compiles with `EmitCWith(…, AllInstances)`.

## Policy and implementation

D-229 specifies nonempty ASCII-decimal CL (surrounding SP/HTAB only), no repeated fields (even equal or zero), no comma lists or signs, and no arbitrary leading-zero digit limit within the existing head cap. Accumulation saturates at MAX_BODY+1 while checking all remaining bytes. Valid larger lengths alone are 413, including integer-overflow numerals; malformed syntax remains 400 even after saturation.

Only one TE field equal to `chunked` is implemented (case-insensitive with surrounding SP/HTAB). Unsupported chains/values and repeated TE are 400. CL+TE is 400 in either order, even for zero or oversized CL. Validation precedes body handling, 100 Continue and handler dispatch. Existing `send_error` sends an empty final error with `connection: close`, stops reads, then closes after writing it; buffered pipelined followers are not dispatched.

The production diff is confined to `pts/runtime/src/http.c`'s header parser. No shared chunk decoder, fetch response parser, line-ending or lifecycle change is made. Node's acceptance of codings before final chunked and its 400 for overflowing CL differ intentionally from this supported-protocol policy. No proxy exploit is claimed.
